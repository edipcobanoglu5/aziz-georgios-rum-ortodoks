Our church

A place of faith in Hatay's shared cultural landscape

Saint George Greek Orthodox Church is part of the historic Christian heritage of Sarılar, Altınözü.

Our story

Overview of Our Community

The church is a meaningful spiritual landmark for its community and for visitors seeking to understand the many layers of Hatay's heritage.

Its story is also one of care and continuity. We welcome respectful visitors and ask everyone to help protect the calm, dignity and historic fabric of this sacred place.

✢

Heritage note

Public reporting describes the church as a 14th-century monument that suffered severe earthquake damage in 2023. Restoration assessment and preservation work remain important to its future.
