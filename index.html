The project

A church at the heart of community life

Built around 1364 and dedicated to Saint George, the church is also known locally as Kaddis Mar Circos. It is the setting for worship and for Easter, Christmas, Palm Sunday, Holy Week, baptisms, weddings, funerals and memorial services.

Why restoration is urgent

The 2023 earthquakes severely damaged the church

The building is partially collapsed. Services are currently held in the third-floor condolence and event room of the adjacent building, where capacity is limited.

1364

Historic church

Estimated construction date: 1364. Its central arch records repairs completed in 1881 following the 1872 earthquake.

360

Local community

The Sarılar community comprises 360 households and approximately 1,500 people.

20K

Global ties

Around 20,000 community members living abroad remain connected to the homeland of their families.

Safeguarding heritage

Restoration will sustain faith, culture and diversity

The project seeks to preserve a vital place of worship, support the continuity of the community's cultural practices, and contribute to the region's cultural tourism and multicultural life.
